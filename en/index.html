Four Pillars of the gut · a clinical frame

Your chronic symptoms may not be failing to heal — you may be feeding them, daily.

Bloating, bad breath, brain fog, cravings, skin problems — scattered symptoms that often share one upstream source: bacterial overgrowth in the gut. And what fed those bacteria was frequently the food you were taught was "healthy." A clinical frame built on eight years of directly measuring patients' breath gases in Seoul.

Who writes this

Eight years of measuring the gas, not guessing at it.
