Neo-Skepticism · Bloating & Gas
Symptom deep-dive · Bloating

Told your gas is just a "sensitive gut"? The smell tells you the answer.

Bloating and flatulence that gets worse the more fiber and probiotics you take — a common misunderstanding. Odorless gas and foul gas are entirely different signals, and the smelly kind points to overgrowth of specific bacteria (SRB).

Read the essays on Substack
1 · There are two kinds of gas

Odorless gas and foul gas are different stories.

Not all gas is the same. The problem is gas that smells.

Common by-product

Odorless gas

H₂ · CH₄

Hydrogen and methane. Common by-products of fermentation, with almost no smell.

Overgrowth signal

Foul gas — SRB

H₂S · CH₃SH

Hydrogen sulfide and methyl mercaptan. A rotten-egg / garlic smell. The signal of sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB) overgrowth.

rotten egggarlic · onion
Key

If your flatulence and body odor carry a clear foul (rotten-egg) smell, it may not be simply "a lot of gas" but SRB overgrowth — especially if it worsened as you added more fiber and probiotics.

2 · What SRB feeds on

The "healthy" foods were the fuel.

SRB overgrows on the combination of sulfur + poorly-digestible substrate. The items below are recommended as healthy, but in this frame they are named as fermentation fuel.

Dietary patternWhy it becomes substrate
Garlic · onion · cruciferousSupplies plant sulfur directly
Protein + fiber togetherProtein not fully absorbed in the small bowel reaches the colon → SRB food
Whole grainsPoorly-digestible substrate prolongs fermentation
Fermented foods (kimchi, yogurt, cheese)Adds external bacteria and fungi directly
Keto · carnivoreExcess protein + sulfur → the same result
In one line

No single diet is the answer — whichever diet it is, if it fails to reduce inflammation, SRB overgrows.

3 · Bloating is not the end but the start

The same gas spreads through the whole body.

Dismiss bloating as "a sensitive gut" and you miss the upstream. The sulfur gas SRB makes does not stay in the belly.

GLP-1 suppression → appetite & impulse

When the satiety hormone is suppressed, appetite and impulse control get harder.

Re-inhaled gas → anxiety & brain fog

Re-inhaling your own gas daily disturbs the emotional center and brain energy.

That's why scattered symptoms come together

It is why bloating arrives alongside anxiety, fatigue, and appetite problems.
